Boosting Mental Health Literacy in Chinese Universities: A Randomized Controlled Trial of Intervention Efficacy

P. Wu†

Social Science and Medicine 2026

This study employed a randomized controlled trial (RCT) design. Using longitudinal pre-post assessments of intervention and control cohorts of first-year students and mixed linear modeling, we examined: (1) the causal effect of the intervention on mental health literacy development; (2) whether improvements in mental health literacy are accompanied by changes in resilience, life satisfaction, and depression; and (3) whether demographic characteristics and baseline psychological functioning moderate these effects.

Gender Differences in Affective and Neural Responses to Teacher Praise and Criticism in University Students: An fNIRS Study

P. Wu†

International Journal of Psychology 2026

The present study employed functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS) to investigate gender differences in neural activation and functional connectivity in response to teachers' evaluative language with different emotional valences (praise, criticism, and neutral statements), while controlling for individual differences in self-esteem and depression.

The neural mechanism of communication between graduate students and advisers in different adviser-advisee relationships

Y. Zhang†

Scientific Reports 2024

This study combines experimental paradigms and brain neuroscience research methods to verify the relationship between both and deepens the mutual influence mechanism of adviser-advisee communication and the learning guidance relationship to the brain nerve level. This allows for a more comprehensive understanding of the cognitive characteristics and neural mechanism of the pros and cons of learning guidance relationship in the communication process.
